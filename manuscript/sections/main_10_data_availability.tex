\section*{Data availability statement}
The milk data are available from CORA Repositori de Dades de Recerca
under DOI \href{https://doi.org/10.34810/data2338}{10.34810/data2338}
with a CC BY-NC-ND 4.0 licence. The analysis code, eligibility rules,
configuration and aggregate numerical checks are available at
\url{https://github.com/Stork343/jpDMEQR}. The replication scripts
retrieve the original data from the source repository and generate
the analysis cohort and manuscript tables.
