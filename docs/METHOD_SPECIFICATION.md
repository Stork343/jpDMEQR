# Clinical conditional quantile estimation

The active research direction is conditional quantile estimation for independent patients with repeated measurements. The user authorised this change on 2026-11-05. The mathematical specification is `research/clinical_quantile_20261105/METHOD.md`. Implementation and empirical checks are versioned under that directory.

The scientific parameter is defined by the conditional outcome distribution. Regularisation and working correlation are estimation choices. They do not enter the definition of the parameter.

`CQ-ENET` fits patient weighted quantile regression with unpenalised prespecified clinical covariates and an elastic net penalty on the remaining covariates. `CQ-ORTH` uses a patient level orthogonal score for a prespecified coefficient. Its working covariance acts on quantile scores. Its validity requires the conditional moment and nuisance rate conditions stated in the specification.

Numerical verification uses actual discovery patients and deterministic mathematical calculations. Empirical tuning uses patient folds. The reserved validation cohorts remain assigned to independent validation after the revised procedure and clinical question have been fixed.

The archived profile method and its corresponding simulations are defined in `docs/methods/joint_profile_20261026.md`. Results generated by that method retain their recorded target and method identifier. The manuscript sources associated with those results describe that version.

Current acceptance requires verification of the weighted optimisation objective, clinical design rank, complete patient fold separation and the orthogonal score identities. A claim of improved prediction or inference requires the relevant comparisons to pass; an implementation alone does not establish either claim.
