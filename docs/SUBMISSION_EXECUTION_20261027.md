## Application comparator calculation

The cohort, outcome, module membership and subject folds remain those of the passed GSE65391 audit. All three processing routes use two clinical models, three quantiles and five repetitions of five subject folds. The three comparator algorithms retain their published or maintained-package objectives and tuning rules. Pooled quantile Lasso has twelve inner candidates from screening dimensions 100, 200 and 400 and penalty multipliers 0.25, 0.5, 1 and 2. QGEE-SCAD has the same three screening dimensions and its official thirty-point HBIC penalty path. LQMM uses the clinical and module design with seven quadrature points.

Continuous clinical predictors are expressed in training-sample standard deviation units for prediction algorithms. Their training means and scales are saved and applied to heldout subjects. Indicator columns and the intercept retain their encoding. This specifies the predictor units entering the induced-smoothing calculation. Gene and module transformations retain their frozen training rules. The complete-profile prediction ridge and orthogonal search depend on the selected prediction span and are invariant to invertible changes of coordinates within the always-included clinical span; this equivalence is checked numerically before reusing completed joint-profile predictions.

QGEE-SCAD uses the maintained `geeVerse` kernel with `standardize=FALSE` on this explicitly transformed design. Its HBIC is thereby evaluated in the same coordinate system as its fitted predictor. The convergence tolerance is unchanged at 1e-4, with an iteration budget of 1000. Its thirty-point penalty sequence and exchangeable working correlation remain fixed. The kernel's selected HBIC fit must converge. Inner model-size candidates must have complete converged fits in every inner fold to be eligible; a job with no such candidate records an estimation failure. LQMM package convergence output and every native warning remain in the records.

The reference preprocessing cache is built from the same processed arrays and sample roles as the completed joint-profile predictions. The alternative-processing caches already contain those exact roles. No heldout outcome enters preprocessing, screening or candidate selection. Final paired comparisons use identical heldout subjects and preserve available and unavailable folds separately.

LQMM retains its likelihood tolerance of 1e-6 and uses numerical iteration budgets of 2000 for the lower loop and 100 for the upper loop. Both recorded loop codes must indicate convergence. The package's negative loop codes remain numerical failures in the comparison accounting.

The source commit recorded when a queue is created identifies its computational version; subsequent manuscript-only commits do not alter that identity.

The other configurations use multiplier one. The true-support diagnostic fits the generated active slopes and requested controls on the estimation subjects. The known-nuisance diagnostic keeps the practical selected designs and removes the generated cluster contribution before coefficient fitting. The population-direction diagnostic applies the independently constructed full-design direction to each constituent fitted score and uses the cluster linearisation of that complete adjustment. Independent-screening settings retain disjoint screening and analysis subjects and run the proposed procedure on the analysis subjects.

Global coefficient errors include every registered slope, including variables omitted by preprocessing. Interval targets remain method-specific. Marginal procedures include their ordinary unpenalised fixed intercept, and their predictions retain it. For pooled quantile Lasso, the multiplier grid applies to the raw-sum penalty scale `sqrt(tau*(1-tau)*N*log(p))`. This is the raw-objective form of the usual mean-loss calibration. Tuning uses the same five subject folds and every registered multiplier.

The comparator numerical specification is `config/submission_comparator_numerics_20261028.yml`. DOUBLE-PEN-QLMM uses the source raw-sum objective and alternating SIC selection. Its fixed-effect penalty scale is `sqrt(tau*(1-tau)*N*log(p))`, and its cluster-effect scale is `sqrt(tau*(1-tau)*(N/n)*log(n*q))`. The factors account for the number of observations entering each score block. The fixed-effect multiplier set is 0.25, 0.5, 1 and 2; the cluster-effect set is 0.5 and 1. The source paper leaves the grid endpoint and length to the implementation. Both scales and both complete grids are saved for each fit before any comparative simulation is summarised.

## Precision calculation

The SQR comparison retains its complete symmetrised CLIME matrix. Each returned row satisfies the original full-design inequalities and a primal-dual optimality check. A native parametric simplex path supplies candidate bases at gamma below 0.25; a quadratic working problem supplies candidates at larger gamma. Any required linear-programme refinement includes all coefficient variables, and violated constraints are added until the full certificate is satisfied. The native path has at most four times the design dimension in pivots. Its interface uses the archived fastclime version 1.4.1.1. The quadratic and linear programmes use osqp and HiGHS, respectively.

The five-fold precision validation loss is nonnegative. Candidate evaluation can therefore end when the accumulated loss divided by five exceeds the best complete candidate's loss, including its tie tolerance. This certifies that the candidate cannot be selected. A failed fold also prevents that candidate from meeting the complete-fold rule. Every configured multiplier retains its evaluated folds and its explicit elimination record. The selected multiplier has all five validation losses. Prediction-only initial fits retain their coefficients and numerical diagnostics; their unused Hessians are omitted.

SQR replication files retain every candidate coefficient vector, subject role, validation loss and selection decision. They store the initial and final rows of each initial-estimator iteration history, together with its iteration count, accepted-step count, objective minimum and numerical-residual summaries. The final inferential score and all requested precision rows remain available in full. This storage representation is checked against completed replication objects by exact equality of the statistical outputs and tuning decisions. It changes the saved diagnostics without changing any fitted quantity.

## Application penalty paths

Every QGEE penalty is an independent fitting attempt. A numerical error makes that penalty unavailable, and the finite HBIC values determine the selected penalty. That selected fit must converge. An input-design cache is used only after the clinical units and subject roles have been checked. Cached predictions are recomputed from the current design matrix before their validation losses are reused. The final QGEE queue contains the complete 450 registered application settings and records the individual-penalty rule explicitly.
